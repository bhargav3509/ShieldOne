Motivation & Overview

Problem Statement & Proposed Solution

Current Industry Challenges
  • • Continuous Monitoring: Cyber threats require 24/7 streaming detection rather than periodic log reviews.
  • • Fragmented Feeds: Intelligence data is dispersed across disparate, uncoordinated external repositories.
  • • Prioritization Bottleneck: Raw indicator logs lack context, geolocation mapping, and severity scoring.
  • • Analyst Fatigue: Security teams need rapid threat interpretation to reduce MTTD and MTTR.
The ShieldOne Platform
  • • Unified Ingestion: Collects multi-source threat feeds in real time.
  • • Global Visualization: Maps active attack telemetry and geographical distribution.
  • • Incident Escalation: Routes critical security events to analyst workflows.
  • • AI Explanation: Utilizes LLM intelligence for instant threat context and mitigation.
Core Objective: Delivering immediate defensive awareness from raw, unstructured threat streams.
